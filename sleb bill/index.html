SECTION 3: MAINTENANCE & SERVICE SCHEDULE

3.1 Free Service Benefits

  • First 3 Services are completely FREE for all customers.
  • Free services include labor, and all inspection charges.
  • Only labor is free after 2 months of delivery; spare parts will be charged separately at actual spare price.

3.2 Mandatory Service Schedule

Your vehicle must be serviced every:

  • 1,500 KM traveled, OR
  • 3 months from the date of delivery
  • Whichever comes FIRST
Service Number Status Condition

SECTION 4: WARRANTY & GUARANTEE COVERAGE

4.1 Covered Components

The warranty and guarantee cover 4 critical parts only:

  1. Motor - Complete motor assembly
  2. Controller - Electronic controller unit
  3. Battery - Lithium-ion battery pack
  4. Charger - Original charging unit

4.2 Coverage Details

  • Manufacturing defects in the above components will be repaired or replaced free of charge during the warranty/guarantee period.
  • Replacement parts will be provided as per ADMS quality standards.
  • Labor charges are waived during the warranty/guarantee period for covered components.

SECTION 5: SPARE PARTS POLICY

5.1 Spare Parts After 2 Months of Delivery

If any spare parts other than the 4 covered components (Motor, Controller, Battery, Charger) stop working or need replacement:

  • After 2 months of delivery: Parts will be charged based on the actual spare price.
  • Labor charges are FREE during the free service period (first 3 services).
  • Examples of non-covered spare parts: Tires, Brakes, Brake Pads, Suspension components, Lights, Wiring, Connectors, etc.

5.2 Labor Charges Policy

  • Free labor: During the first 3 free services and throughout the warranty/guarantee period for covered components.
  • Paid labor: After the 3 free services are exhausted, labor charges will apply as per the service center's standard rates.

SECTION 6: IMPORTANT - WARRANTY & GUARANTEE FORFEITURE CLAUSE

WARRANTY AND GUARANTEE WILL BE VOID IF:

  1. Any scheduled service is missed or delayed - If the customer does not service the vehicle within the prescribed 1,500 KM / 3 months schedule (whichever comes first), the warranty and guarantee become NULL AND VOID.
  2. Any free service is not availed - Skipping even one of the first 3 free services will result in forfeiture of all warranty and guarantee benefits.
  3. Service done from unauthorized centers - Any servicing or repairs done outside of authorized ADMS service centers will void the warranty and guarantee.
  4. Tampering with vehicle components - Any unauthorized modification, disassembly, or tampering with the vehicle will cancel all warranty and guarantee coverage.
  1. Improper usage - Misuse, overloading beyond capacity, or operation in extreme conditions will void coverage.
  2. Battery misuse - Using non-original chargers or batteries will invalidate all warranty.

Once warranty/guarantee is forfeited due to missed services, it CANNOT be restored. Regular service is MANDATORY to maintain coverage.

SECTION 7: CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITIES

The customer agrees with:

  • ✓ Follow the mandatory service schedule (every 1,500 KM or 3 months, whichever is earlier).
  • ✓ Get the vehicle serviced only at authorized ADMS service centers.
  • ✓ Use only original ADMS chargers and batteries.
  • ✓ Maintain the vehicle as per the user manual guidelines.
  • ✓ Keep the vehicle within permissible load limits.
  • ✓ Report any defects or issues to the authorized service center promptly.
  • ✓ Retain this signed document and service records for all warranty claims.

SECTION 8: EXCLUSIONS (NOT COVERED UNDER WARRANTY/GUARANTEE)

The following are NOT covered under warranty or guarantee:

  • • Normal wear and tears (tires, brake pads, chains, etc.).
  • • Cosmetic damage, scratches, or paint damage.
  • • Damage due to accidents, collisions, or misuse.
  • • Water damage or corrosion due to improper storage.
  • • Electrical faults due to lightning or power surges.
  • • Damage from unauthorized repairs or modifications.
  • • Battery degradation beyond manufacturer specifications.
  • • Lost or stolen parts or components.
  • • Damage due to using incompatible accessories.

SECTION 9: CLAIM PROCESS FOR WARRANTY/GUARANTEE

To claim warranty or guarantee benefits:

  1. Contact the authorized ADMS service center with your vehicle and this signed document.
  2. Provide proof of purchase (original invoice/bill).
  3. Present this signed Terms & Conditions document showing your warranty/guarantee period.
  4. Provide service history showing all scheduled services were completed on time.
  5. The service center will assess the defect and proceed with repair/replacement if eligible.
  6. All claims must be made within the warranty/guarantee period - claims after expiry will not be entertained.

SECTION 10: GENERAL TERMS

  • This agreement is valid only for the original purchaser (non-transferable).
  • The customer must keep this document safe for future reference and claim processing.
  • Any disputes will be resolved as per ADMS policies and applicable Indian laws.
  • ADMS reserves the right to change warranty terms with prior notice.
